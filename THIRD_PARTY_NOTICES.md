# Third-Party Notices

Project is private and non-commercial.

## Pokémon FireRed / LeafGreen environment assets

`godot-digital-world/assets/sprites/environment/` and
`godot-digital-world/source/maps/overworld/` include environment assets and
map scenes from the open-source Godot 4.4 fan project:

- https://github.com/SlayHorizon/pokemon-fireleaf-godot
- Code: MIT License
- The project states its art assets come from the original Pokémon FireRed and
  LeafGreen games and are used for non-commercial educational/technical
  exploration. No Pokémon characters are used in our scenes.

## Digital Partner 1.1.9

`digitalpartner_sprites/` and `digitalpartner_backgrounds/` contain pixel
sprites and background references extracted from the user-provided
`DigitalPartner1-1-9(PC).exe` GameMaker build:

- `data.win` → `SPRT` / `TPAG` / `TXTR` / `STRG` parsing
- Original `digimon_data.json`, `license.txt`, and `Gigimon`/Digimon pixel
  assets are retained for this private project.
